Why now

The attacks aren’t waiting for you to hire.

Small and mid-sized companies face the same adversaries as the enterprise — with none of the staff. These are the four fires ClearFend was built to answer, in plain language.

Ransomware

One phished password or unpatched box, and your files — and your business — are held hostage. It is the dominant threat to companies your size.

AI-era attacks

Attackers automate reconnaissance and phishing with AI — and the AI you’ve deployed becomes its own new surface to defend.

Exposed to the internet

A forgotten server, an open admin panel, a leaked key. You can’t defend what you can’t see — and no one is looking.

Deals that stall

Questions

The questions a careful buyer asks.

Straight answers. If we don’t know something yet, we say so — that’s the whole brand.

Do you change anything in our environment?
No. ClearFend connects with read-only access and reads your configuration — it doesn’t write to it. Break-in Testing exercises your public surface safely, the way an adversary would probe it, without disrupting your systems.
Is the security number just an AI guess?
No. A deterministic engine — not the AI — authors every finding and rating from your evidence. The AI explains findings and proposes fixes, and a human on your side approves what matters. When evidence is missing, the check reads Unknown, never a green pass.
Does this make us certified or audit-ready?
We’re careful here: ClearFend is built to align with frameworks like SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS and NIST, and is CIS-referenced. It stands up and runs the program and assembles the evidence — but a certification is issued by an accredited auditor, not by us. ClearFend gets you ready and gives you the proof to hand them.
We have no security person. Can we run this?
That’s exactly who it’s for. The agents do the continuous work; the console is plain English; and Ask ClearFend answers questions about your own posture. You approve decisions — you don’t have to be the analyst.
How is this different from Vanta, Drata, or an MSSP?
Compliance platforms help you run one framework yourself; an MSSP monitors under a retainer. ClearFend is a team that does both — plus AppSec, Secure by Design, and Break-in Testing — and unifies it into one honest number with the proof attached. If you already run a tool that’s working, we’ll tell you plainly whether you need us yet.
Can we really stand up a program in an hour?
Yes. You pick your industry, connect read-only, and a full program — controls, ownership, and a ranked fix queue — is generated and scored against your live evidence in about an hour. Closing the gaps takes longer; knowing exactly where you stand does not.
