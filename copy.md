## Section 2 — Why now (the threats, named plainly)

Header: **The attacks aren't waiting for you to hire.** SMBs face the same adversaries as the enterprise with none of the staff. Four fires, in plain language:

1. **Ransomware** — one phished password or unpatched box and your business is held hostage. The dominant threat to companies this size.
2. **AI-era attacks** — attackers automate recon and phishing with AI; the AI you've deployed is its own new surface to defend.
3. **Exposed to the internet** — a forgotten server, an open admin panel, a leaked key. You can't defend what you can't see.
4. **Deals that stall** — a security questionnaire or audit lands and the deal freezes while you scramble for answers you don't have.

**Honesty note on the page:** we name these plainly and don't dress them up with borrowed statistics. The promise isn't fear — it's a team that watches all four every day and shows you exactly where you stand. _(No numeric stats are asserted here; any future stat must be real, attributable, and sourced on-page.)_

## Section 4 — What you get (the four offerings)

Header: **Four security programs. One console. One number.** Each is a real, named program — the kind you'd hire a specialist to run.

1. **Secure by Design** — *Bake security into every launch before it ships.* Outcome: fewer incidents born in the roadmap. For: teams shipping product who can't staff a security architect.
2. **Application Security** — *Catch vulnerabilities in your code and cloud, continuously.* Outcome: vulnerabilities fixed before they ship, with a receipt for each one closed. For: anyone running code in the cloud without an AppSec engineer.
3. **Break-in Testing** — *We safely attack your public surface like an adversary and prove what holds.* The pen test that used to be a five-figure engagement, on a schedule and re-run on change. Outcome: proof your defenses work, not a hopeful assumption. For: anyone with anything exposed to the internet.
4. **GRC & the ClearFend Benchmark** — *Pick your industry and get a compliance program from scratch in an hour, not months.* Built to align with SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS and NIST, and CIS-referenced. Outcome: a program you can prove to an auditor, an insurer, or a customer's questionnaire. For: every paid tier.

**Unified by:** the **Confidence number** (all four roll into one honest score with the proof one click away), the **autonomous agents** (run across every program at once), and **Ask ClearFend** (ask your posture anything, get a cited answer).

## Section 6 — How it works (a program in an hour)

Header: **A security program, standing up in an hour.** No consultants, no six-month rollout, no binder of policies to write.

1. **Pick your industry** (minutes) — ClearFend selects the frameworks that apply; built to align with SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, NIST, CIS-referenced.
2. **Connect read-only** (minutes) — link identity, cloud, and code; agents start mapping immediately; nothing is changed in your environment.
3. **Get your program** (same hour) — a full program is generated from scratch — controls, ownership, ranked fix queue — scored against live evidence, not a blank template.
4. **Prove it, on demand** (ongoing) — hand your Confidence number and its evidence to a board, an insurer, or a customer's questionnaire; the agents keep it current.

Badge: **From industry to a scored program in about 1 hour** — what used to take months.

## Section 9 — FAQ (straight answers)

1. **Do you change anything in our environment?** No — read-only. Break-in Testing exercises your public surface safely.
2. **Is the number just an AI guess?** No — a deterministic engine authors every rating; missing evidence reads Unknown, never a green pass.
3. **Does this make us certified / audit-ready?** ClearFend is *built to align with* frameworks and gets you ready with the proof to hand an accredited auditor; certification is issued by the auditor, not by us.
4. **We have no security person — can we run this?** That's exactly who it's for. Agents do the work; the console is plain English; Ask ClearFend answers; you approve.
5. **How is this different from Vanta / Drata / an MSSP?** Those help you run one framework, or monitor under a retainer. ClearFend is a team that does both plus AppSec, Secure by Design, and Break-in Testing, unified into one honest number with proof.
6. **Can we really stand up a program in an hour?** Yes — knowing where you stand takes an hour; closing the gaps takes longer.
7. **Is it available today?** Private preview. Join the waitlist. Everything on the page describes what ClearFend does — no claimed customers or results we don't have.
