Straight answers

Questions a skeptic would ask

How new are you, really? Do you have customers?

We’re at the start, and we won’t pretend otherwise. The assessment pipeline — the scanners, the translation layer, the report — is built and running today. The platform is in development, and this page labels which is which everywhere. You’ll notice there are no logos, no testimonials, and no “trusted by” wall here: when we have numbers worth showing, we’ll publish them.

What you get for being early: fixed pricing in writing, direct access to the people building the product, and design-partner priority when continuous monitoring ships.

Is the assessment an audit, a pentest, or a certification?

No, no, and no — and we put that in the report, on this page, and on the delivery call. It’s a point-in-time posture assessment: findings are configuration-based, from read-only access only. It tells you where you stand and what to fix before you pay an auditor or a pentester — it doesn’t replace either.

Why can the assessment only award Level 2?

Because Level 3 — Audit-Ready — means controls proven continuously, and no snapshot can honestly claim that. An assessor who awards “audit-ready” off a one-day scan is selling you a number that expires overnight. We’d rather cap the score honestly and tell you exactly what continuous proof requires. Yes, that continuous monitoring is what our platform will sell — we’re telling you the incentive out loud so you can judge the reasoning on its merits.

What access do you need, and what happens to it?

Time-boxed, read-only access that you create with us on a 30-minute call — we never ask for your passwords and we never change anything in your environment. The scanners are vetted open-source tools (CISA SCuBA baselines, CIS Foundations checks). Access revocation begins the day we deliver, and the report notes it.

Why not just use Vanta or Drata?

If checkbox-compliance automation is all you’ll ever need, they’re competent products. But they only work the paperwork side — they don’t harden your systems, run your security operations, or talk to your insurer — and their renewal pricing is notorious (30–50% hikes are widely reported). ClearFend’s thesis is one loop: fix the problem and the proof generates itself, with renewal caps written into the contract. Today, our assessment gives you the SOC 2 gap view before you commit to anyone — including us.

Why not Huntress, or a bundle like Coro?

Huntress is genuinely great at managed detection — if that’s your only gap, buy it with our blessing. It meters per endpoint (three devices, three charges) and doesn’t close the compliance or insurance loop. The all-in-one bundles (Coro, Guardz, Cynet) staple point modules together — you get many dashboards under one invoice, not one graph with one queue and proof as the output. Our bet is the loop, not the bundle. Judge us on it when the platform ships; judge the assessment on its own merits today.

Isn’t Microsoft enough? I already pay for Business Premium.

Keep it — seriously. Business Premium includes strong tools, but the tenant ships insecure-by-default (CISA’s SCuBA hardening baselines exist precisely because of this), nobody triages the alerts, and nothing in it produces SOC 2 evidence or insurer answers. We’re the layer that hardens, operates, and proves — and when the platform launches, the Defender you already own slots in as a first-class engine, not a duplicate you pay for twice. Complement, not rip-and-replace.

Who is the assessment for?

Companies of roughly 25–250 people running Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace (plus Okta and/or AWS/Azure/GCP) with a live trigger: a SOC 2 request from a customer, a cyber-insurance renewal or application, or a board asking “are we OK?” If that’s you, the five-day report is priced to pay for itself in audit-prep hours alone.

You fix things — the report is ordered so the first hour of work buys the most safety, and many fixes are 20-minute settings changes. And honestly: your report is 90 days stale the day we email it. Continuous monitoring keeps it true — that’s ClearFend Starter at platform launch, and assessment customers get first access as design partners.
